# AI architecture roadmap

AI is not enabled in Phase 1. Later phases will first detect clear revenue conditions with deterministic rules, then optionally use a provider abstraction for structured explanations and message drafts. Retrieval must filter by workspace before ranking. All proposed actions will pass schema validation, policy checks, confidence thresholds, and human approval before the mock executor can run. Model, prompt version, context references, validation outcome, and execution outcome will be auditable without storing unnecessary secrets.
